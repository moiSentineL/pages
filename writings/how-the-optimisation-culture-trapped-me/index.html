How the optimisation culture trapped me

There was a time when I used to optimise the shit out of my life. I used to think it was “noble” and “great” to try juggle 20 things at the same time, trying to fill every part of my day, be busy, be stressed, neglect my relationships, never go out of my house.

Some societal trends like “hustle culture”, “productivity culture” and the influence of certain rationalist circles (which were my reference group back then) made me believe that if I wasn’t doing something cool and effectively ‘huge’ at a particular time, I was being immoral and decrepit.

All because of supposed “greatness” and because I didn’t want to remain mediocre for long, which was fine as a goal. But what fucked me was how I tried executing and achieving the goal.
